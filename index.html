How to play

Climb a staircase of words: one letter at the top, one more on each step. Easy has 5 steps, Intermediate 6 and Hard 7. Every row and column of two or more letters is a real word. The single squares at the top and bottom-right corner belong only to the long word running through them.

Tap a square to pick its row, tap again for its column, then type. Letters you've already found stay filled in, so you only type the empty squares.

steam

Blue means right letter, right square. Found letters stay on the board and help the words that cross them.

craner

Pink with a dot means the letter is in this word, in another square. It's also added to that word's log: rows keep theirs to the left of the grid, columns keep theirs underneath.

rr

A logged letter turns red when a row and a column that cross at an open square both have it. That's a strong hint it belongs where they meet, so that square gets a red outline. Once you place a letter, it leaves the log.

blunt

Dimmed means the letter isn't in this word, though it may be in another.

tube

While you type, a letter you've already ruled out for that square turns orange. You can still check the word.

  • Each difficulty has its own daily puzzle, plus random puzzles any time. Tap the logo (or press Back) to return to the title screen.
  • On a computer, just type. Tab moves to the next word, Shift+Space switches direction, Enter checks.
  • Correct words are free. You can miss 12 times on Easy, 15 on Intermediate and 18 on Hard.
  • Letters you type but don't check stay as faint blueprint letters when you move to another word, so you can sketch ideas across the grid. Press Space to skip a square and leave it blank. Come back to finish and check them, or Backspace to erase.
  • Each puzzle comes with 3 hints. A hint fills in the letter at your cursor in the selected row or column, and doesn't count as a miss.
