# Hidden: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 26, 2026*

Hidden is a word puzzle game you can play in the Android app or in a web browser. This policy explains what happens to your information in each.

## The Android app

The app collects nothing. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data.

- The app has no internet permission and never connects to the internet.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.
- Your progress, results and settings (difficulty, theme, sound) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## The web version

The web version counts plays anonymously with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service, so we can see how many people play and how hard the puzzles are.

- **What's counted:** that the page was visited (with the referring site, browser type, screen size and country, which GoatCounter works out from the request), plus anonymous game events: starting a puzzle, which difficulty, whether it was won or given up, the number of misses and hints, and sharing a result.
- **What isn't:** no names, accounts, email addresses or the words you type. No cookies are used, and GoatCounter doesn't store your IP address or track you across sites.
- Your progress and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

## Sharing your results

When you tap **Share**, the app opens Android's share sheet with a short text summary of your result (for example, the coloured grid and your number of misses). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with. After that, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

Hidden is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
